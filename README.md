## About me

I'm a UK-based **DevOps Engineer with over two years of professional experience** across cloud infrastructure, deployment automation and platform operations. I've worked with teams at **DRIFE**, **Carrier Abound** and the **University of Hertfordshire**, combining day-to-day engineering work with practical research into secure delivery.

I focus on **AWS, Terraform, Kubernetes and CI/CD**, with a growing interest in **site reliability engineering**, observability and automating operational work. I enjoy troubleshooting issues, understanding why systems fail and making the next deployment easier to operate.
